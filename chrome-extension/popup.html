THE SCREAMING VOLUME SLIDER™

On YouTube (and other sites), changing volume requires a scream. Your scream average becomes the video volume.

  1. Open YouTube (or any video site).
  2. Try to change the volume.
  3. Scream when the overlay appears.
  4. Volume = your scream average.

Mic is used only during scream mode. Audio is analyzed locally — recordings are never stored.
